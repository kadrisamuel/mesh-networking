  ## Findings

  1. HIGH — Group-wide AES-GCM nonce uniqueness cannot be
     enforced.
     Every group member derives the same epoch outer_key,
     but nonce reservations and the 2^24 limit are local
     to each device. Receivers explicitly do not record
     nonce reservations. Sixteen members could
     independently produce 2^28 encryptions under one
     key, with an approximate random-nonce collision
     probability of 2^-41. Use per-sender outer keys or
     another reviewed nonce-partitioning construction.
     docs/spec/CRYPTOGRAPHY_V1.md:214, docs/spec/
     CRYPTOGRAPHY_V1.md:232, docs/spec/THREAT_MODEL.md:97

  2. HIGH — The 16-member group requirement cannot fit
     the bootstrap limits.
     The checked-in MLS BasicCredential identity is 199
     bytes. Sixteen credentials plus mandatory 64-byte
     leaf signatures alone require at least 4,208 bytes—
     before keys, tree nodes, Welcome, COSE, HPKE, or
     envelope overhead. This exceeds both the 2,000-byte
     bootstrap COSE cap and 4,096-byte envelope cap. The
     required ratchet-tree extension makes the
     contradiction unavoidable.
     docs/spec/CRYPTOGRAPHY_V1.md:124, docs/spec/
     CRYPTOGRAPHY_V1.md:198, docs/spec/PROTOCOL_V1.md:16,
     docs/spec/PROTOCOL_V1.md:181

  3. MEDIUM — All-zero recovery entropy should fail
     closed.
     Under an ideal uniform sampler it is valid with
     probability 2^-256, but accepting it defeats a
     nearly free check for zero-filled buffers or a
     catastrophically stuck RNG. It produces the
     published mnemonic and root in the vectors. Reject
     all-zero recovery entropy and fail after bounded
     retries; preferably apply the same sentinel check to
     every security-critical random key. The all-zero
     case can remain a negative derivation fixture.
     docs/spec/CRYPTOGRAPHY_V1.md:53, docs/spec/
     CRYPTOGRAPHY_V1.md:57, docs/spec/vectors/v1/
     vectors.json:95

  4. MEDIUM — Routing-tag overlap omits valid oldest
     slots.
     At the start of slot S, a user envelope from S-5 can
     still satisfy expires+10 > W, yet only S-4…S are
     searched. Control traffic similarly needs S-29
     during the first ten minutes, not only S-28…S. Valid
     envelopes can therefore pass time admission but be
     unroutable.
     docs/spec/PROTOCOL_V1.md:121, docs/spec/
     PROTOCOL_V1.md:131, docs/adr/0003-envelope-relay-
     and-transport.md:15

  5. MEDIUM — Direct-chat membership authorization and
     removal transitions are incomplete.
     Direct chats have a null owner, while membership
     authorization is defined only in terms of an owner.
     The specification never explicitly rejects all
     direct-chat membership-changing Commits. It also
     does not define the state entered by a client
     removed from a group.
     docs/spec/CRYPTOGRAPHY_V1.md:210, docs/spec/
     PROTOCOL_V1.md:181, docs/spec/CRYPTOGRAPHY_V1.md:295

  6. MEDIUM — Golden-vector coverage does not meet DEC-
     001’s “every derivation and binary format”
     criterion.
     L_DATABASE_WRAP_AAD, Android wrapping, and Windows
     DPAPI-entropy/record encoding have no vectors.
     Several sync/application variants and LoRa size
     mappings are also absent. Additionally, the only
     KeyPackage/Welcome is deliberately application-
     mismatched; the expected rejection is a hard-coded
     string, not independently validated, and there is no
     positive application-bound bootstrap vector.
     tasks/00_FOUNDATION_AND_DECISIONS.md:22, docs/spec/
     vectors/v1/README.md:42, docs/spec/vectors/v1/
     README.md:48, docs/spec/vectors/v1/
     generate_vectors.py:725

  7. MEDIUM — At least one acceptance sample is not
     executable as written.
     PRO-FRAG-001 requires “all loss/duplicate/order
     patterns”; duplicate patterns are unbounded and no
     finite schedule/count is defined, yet the row has a
     180-second timeout. This fails DEC-001’s exact-
     sample requirement.
     docs/spec/TEST_MATRIX.md:68,
     tasks/00_FOUNDATION_AND_DECISIONS.md:26

  8. LOW — Noise handshake payload encoding is
     unspecified.
     Vectors use empty handshake payloads, but the
     protocol does not require or validate this. A
     conforming implementation could place application
     data in Noise NN’s unauthenticated first flight.
     Require empty payloads and exact handshake message
     lengths.
     docs/spec/PROTOCOL_V1.md:206, docs/spec/
     PROTOCOL_V1.md:249
